The bird’s
fourth primary

Your eye keeps three kinds of cone, so every colour you have ever seen is a mixture of three signals — a point in a cube. The bird at your window keeps four. Its fourth cone drinks ultraviolet, and its colours are mixtures of four signals: a point in a four-dimensional cube no page can hold.

The plate opposite shows that object — every dot one state of the four cones l · m · s · uv. Three of its axes make the cube you live in. The fourth is the one this book cannot print.

Ultraviolet is set in a false colour throughout, there being no true one.
